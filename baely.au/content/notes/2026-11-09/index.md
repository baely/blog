Every single overseas holiday I've taken so far has been to busy cities or interesting regions where there is so much to do that whether or not I'd done any planning beforehand, my days would either be spent out-and-about and active or spent slower and regretting not making the most of my time. Knowing this, I was little unsure how I would feel completely unwinding and doing nothing going into the 9 days of my recent Fiji holiday.

Instead I discovered the true meaning of a vacation by enjoying the sense of weightlessnes that spending a week in resorts provides (although, that weightlessness feeling might have just been from the hammock that I spent half my time snoozing in).
